1. Official Grading Rubrics & Penalties (Oposiciones Andalucía)

A. Written Theme Rubric
  • Structure (10%): Index, Intro, Dev, Conclusion, Bibliography.
  • Specific Contents (60%): Depth, updating, technical/pedagogical terminology.
  • Critical Judgment (10%): Epistemological & normative argumentation.
  • Exemplification (7%): Professional & school context application.
  • Citations (6%): 3+ normative/biblio references.
B. Teaching Program (Written)
  • Justification (3%): Relevance of the didactic planning.
  • Contextualization (3%): Center environment, diversity, student profile.
  • Structure (21%): 13 core sections (Context, Legislation, Curricular Concretion, DUA, Evaluation).
  • ⚠ Penalization (-10 pts): If any unit lacks at least 1 Learning Situation or if program lacks min 12 units[cite: 175].
C. Spelling & General Penalties
  • 1 falta: -0,5 pts. • 2 faltas: -1,5 pts[cite: 174].
  • 3 faltas: -2,5 pts. • 4 faltas: -4,0 pts[cite: 174].
  • 5+ faltas (o tildes incorrectas): -10 puntos (Anexo VII)[cite: 174].
  • -10 pts automáticos: Marcas de identidad, folios no facilitados, no escribir por anverso/reverso en vertical, ejercicio ilegible (Anexo VIII)[cite: 174, 175].
